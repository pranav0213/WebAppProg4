Pranav presents plays by Ibsen

An Enemy of the People

Act 2

Scene

(A knock is heard at the door.) Come in!

(ASLAKSEN appears at the door. He is poorly but decently dressed, in black, with a slightly crumpled white neckcloth; he wears gloves and has a felt hat in his hand.)

Aslaksen

(bowing) Excuse my taking the liberty, Doctor—

Dr. Stockmann

(getting up) Ah, it is you, Aslaksen!

Aslaksen

Yes, Doctor.

Hovstad

(standing up) Is it me you want, Aslaksen?

Aslaksen

No; I didn't know I should find you here. No, it was the Doctor I—

Dr. Stockmann

I am quite at your service. What is it?

Aslaksen

Is what I heard from Mr. Billing true, sir—that you mean to improve our water supply?

Dr. Stockmann

Yes, for the Baths.
